# Security

## Reporting a security issue

If you think you have found a security issue in Skoft Files, please **do not** open a public issue or discussion.

Instead, email [support@skoft.app](mailto:support@skoft.app) and include, where possible:

- a description of the issue and its possible impact;
- the steps to reproduce it;
- your Skoft Files version and operating system.

Please do not include passwords or other people's personal data in your report, and do not share the details publicly.

Thank you for helping keep Skoft Files and its users safe.
